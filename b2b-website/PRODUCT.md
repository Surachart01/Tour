# Vera Thailandia B2B Website

## Product

Vera Thailandia is a Thailand-based inbound Destination Management Company for travel agencies and tour operators. The website presents its on-ground capabilities, gives trade partners useful itinerary inspiration, and routes qualified requests into the agent portal or an RFP conversation.

## Primary Users

- International travel agencies and tour operators evaluating a Thailand ground partner
- Product managers building FIT, group, series, and incentive programs
- Existing agency partners returning to the booking portal

## Core Jobs

- Understand where Vera Thailandia operates and which services it manages
- Review representative Thailand routes and destination coverage
- Create a concise operational brief for a new request
- Request a proposal from the Bangkok operations team
- Enter the existing agent portal

## Confirmed Capabilities

- Tailor-made FIT and group itineraries
- Hotel contracting and accommodation coordination
- Transfers, transport, guides, excursions, and special packages
- English and Italian partner communication
- Local operational support in Thailand
- Dedicated B2B agent portal

## Experience Principles

- Operational clarity before decorative marketing
- Thailand should be visible through real destination photography
- Trade language should be concise, specific, and easy to scan
- Orange is a brand signal, not a page-wide wash
- Every primary action should lead to the portal or a proposal request
